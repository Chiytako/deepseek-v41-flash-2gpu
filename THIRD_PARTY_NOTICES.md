### Data files in `quant/tools/`

- `zh_kanji_ja.json`: the Jōyō kanji list (2,137 entries as extracted) and the Jinmeiyō kanji list (810 characters), extracted from
  Japanese Wikipedia list articles (text CC BY-SA). The lists themselves are official Japanese government lists.
- `zh_words.json`, `zh_only_tokens.json`: generated by `zh_calib.py` / `zh_vocab_r5.py` from the DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash tokenizer
  (MIT) and the corpora listed below (frequency statistics only, no corpus text).

- **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash** weights, tokenizer, `encoding/` and `inference/` reference code (MIT, DeepSeek):

- **Container images**: `ghcr.io/tpurtell/ds41rt-coordinator:v15` (ds41rt), `ghcr.io/local-inference-lab/vllm:release-10152026`
  (Local Inference Lab, <https://github.com/local-inference-lab/blackwell-llm-docker>; based on NVIDIA PyTorch containers, which have their own terms).
- **vLLM / vllm-exl3** (Apache-2.0 / AGPL-3.0) and the nktlabs Engram prestage patches: only used by the A/B scripts
  `quant/build/serve_vllm_exl3.sh` and `build_vllm_exl3_prestage.sh`, which read them from outside this repository.
- **sudoingX dgx-spark-deepseek-v41** (Apache-2.0): calibration data and `alpha_sensitivity.json` read by some analysis tools.
- **Rust crates** used by ds41gw (axum, hyper, tokio, reqwest, tokenizers, serde, and others): fetched from crates.io at build time;
  see `proxy/ds41gw/Cargo.lock`. **Python packages** (openai, anthropic, numpy, torch, tokenizers, ...) are installed separately.
- **Text corpora** (calibration and evaluation; fetched by `quant/tools/fetch_ja_corpus.py` and others, not redistributed):

  `llm-jp/oasst2-33k-ja` (Apache-2.0), `kunishou/databricks-dolly-15k-ja` (CC BY-SA 3.0), JSQuAD / JCommonsenseQA (JGLUE, CC BY-SA 4.0),
  ELYZA-tasks-100 (CC BY-SA 4.0), and other Japanese benchmarks used by `quant/tools/r3ja/` (JHumanEval, MGSM-ja, MMLU-ProX-ja,
  M-IFEval-ja, Japanese MT-Bench, JamC-QA), alpaca-zh, Chinese Wikipedia, Unihan (Unicode License). Each keeps its own license.
- **Image evaluation sets** (fetched by `quant/tools/vl/`, not redistributed): SakanaAI JA-VLM-Bench-In-the-Wild (images: Unsplash License),
  Japanese-Heron-Bench (only PD / CC0 / CC BY 2.0 images), JA-VG-VQA-500 (CC BY 4.0), MECHA-ja (Apache-2.0), JMMMU (MIT; only CC / PD images),
  CC-OCR (MIT), TextVQA (CC BY 4.0; OpenImages CC BY 2.0), MMBench (Apache-2.0; images per source),
  **ChartQA (GPL-3.0)** and **DocVQA (research use only)** — internal evaluation only.
